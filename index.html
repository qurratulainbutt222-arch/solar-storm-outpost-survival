Space Explorer Dictionary

Sol
One day on Mars. It is a little longer than a day on Earth.
Radiation Dose
A way to measure how much energy from radiation a person or place receives.
Microgravity
A condition where things seem almost weightless and can float around.
Solar Flare
A sudden, powerful burst of energy from the Sun.
