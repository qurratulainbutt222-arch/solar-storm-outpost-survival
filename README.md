## 🎯 Project Overview
In **Solar Storm Outpost Survival**, young astronauts manage an off-world Martian habitat (Outpost Ares). Players face consecutive space weather hazards driven by real historical geomagnetic storm events from NASA's DONKI database.

### 🎮 Gameplay Mechanics & Trade-offs
* **Power vs. Safety:** Activating heavy energy shields protects the outpost health from dangerous Coronal Mass Ejections (CMEs), but rapidly drains battery reserves.
* **Daily Resource Management:** Players must balance Oxygen, Power, and Outpost Health across 6 Martian Solar Days (Sols).
* **Mission Advisor ("Why Did I Fail?"):** If resources reach 0, an interactive advisor explains the scientific reason for mission failure, helping kids learn real space risk management.

---

## 📊 Real NASA Open Data Integration
This project utilizes verified historical space weather records from the **NASA DONKI (Database of Notifications, Knowledge, and Information)**:
* **May 2024 Gannon Geomagnetic Storm** (The Great Solar Burp)
* **October 2003 Halloween Solar Storms** (The Halloween Sun Surprise)
* **March 2015 St. Patrick's Day Storm** (The Emerald Sky Storm)
* **September 2017 & March 2012 CMEs**
